Authors, publishers & genres

Managing the system's auxiliary catalogues

Manage the auxiliary catalogues that book records depend on.

Adding an entry

  1. Go to Add New → Authors, publishers & genres and pick the relevant tab.
  2. Click New and fill in the name (and surname, for authors).
  3. Save.

Listings

  • Each tab shows its table with search, pagination and sorting.
  • Use the icons on each row to edit or delete.

Deleting

Deletion runs through a confirmation modal showing the affected entry before the action is executed.

If you try to delete an author, publisher or genre assigned to books, the application will warn you: reassign those books first.
