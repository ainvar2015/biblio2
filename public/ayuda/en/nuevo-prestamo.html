New loan

Recording the handover of a book to a member or external borrower

Record the handover of a book to a member or an external borrower.

Steps

  1. Under Loans → New loan, search for and select the book (only available ones are listed).
  2. Identify the borrower: pick an existing member or fill in the external borrower's details (name, ID or email).
  3. The return date is proposed automatically according to the library's rules; you can adjust it manually if needed.
  4. Confirm the loan.
If the book has pending reservations, the system will tell you: serve the reservation queue first.

Borrower types

TypeWho it is
MemberA user registered under Users with a complete profile.
