Profile

Personal details, password and lock PIN

Personal data of the signed-in user.

What you can do

  • Review and complete your personal details.
  • Change your password: enter the current one and the new one twice to confirm it.
  • Set the screen-lock PIN used by the screen lock feature.
If you forget your password, an administrator can reset it from Users.
Use a password different from the screen-lock PIN: the PIN is a quick helper, but the password protects the actual access.
