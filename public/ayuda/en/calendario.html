Calendar

Loan activity on a monthly view

The Calendar shows scheduled loan activity on a monthly view.

What you can check

  • Due dates of active loans.
  • Loans recorded on each day.
  • Month navigation to plan reminders.

Typical use

  1. Open Calendar from the menu.
  2. Browse to the month you want to review.
  3. Click a day with activity to see the loan details.
Combine the calendar with the email reminders in the loans module to reduce late returns.
