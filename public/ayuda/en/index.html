Introduction, sign-in and manual structure

This application is a library management system: it lets you catalogue books with their authors, publishers and genres, manage loans to members and external borrowers, handle reservations with a waiting queue, and review activity from a dashboard with charts.

How to use this manual

The menu on the left mirrors the application menu, including the dropdown groups (Add New, Edit, Loans and Users). Each page explains what the module is for, how to access it and the usual workflow.

Signing in

  1. Open the application URL and enter your username and password.
  2. After validating, you will be redirected to the Dashboard.
  3. If your session stays idle for a while, the application will ask you to sign in again.
