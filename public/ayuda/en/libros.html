Browse books

General catalogue, search and detail view

Browse books is the general catalogue: search, filter and view each book's full record.

Browsing the catalogue

  • Use the search box to find by title, ISBN or author.
  • Genre or publisher filters narrow the results.
  • The listing shows all authors of each work, comma-separated.

Book detail

  1. Click a book to open its detail window.
  2. You will see the cover, synopsis, authors and availability status.

Covers: the 3 cases

SituationWhat is shown
